Show the day ↓

Your day at a glance

  1. 07:00 – 10:00
    🚆

    Travelling from Budapest

    Train to Miskolc, then local transport to Lillafüred. Start early so you have time to explore the area at your own pace.

  2. 10:00 – 11:30
    🏰

    Palace Hotel and hanging gardens

    Walk around the romantic castle, through the terraces of the hanging gardens and down to the waterfall.

    📸 One of the best photo spots of the day

  3. 11:30 – 12:30
    🕯️

    Cave or forest walk

    If it's open and a guided tour is running, visit St. Stephen's Cave (Szent István-barlang). A good option on cool or rainy days too.

    ☔ Works in the rain · check opening hours in advance

  4. 12:30 – 14:00
    🍲

    Lunch and Lake Hámori

    Lunch at a local restaurant or a simple hot meal, then a hand-in-hand stroll along the lakeshore. If the weather is nice, this may be the most peaceful part of the day.

  5. 14:00 – 15:30
    🚂

    Forest railway or scenic hike

    If the autumn timetable and connections allow, hop on the Lillafüred forest railway for a short woodland adventure. Otherwise, take an easy forest trail.

  6. 15:30 – evening
    ☕

    Coffee, dessert and the trip home

    A hot chocolate or a slice of cake together, then back to Miskolc and the train to Budapest.

    🌙 It gets dark early in autumn, so don't leave too late

Getting there from Budapest

1

Budapest-Keleti → Miskolc-Tiszai

About 2 h 2 min on the direct InterCity, depending on the train you pick.

Tickets and timetable on the MÁV website ↗
→
2

Miskolc-Tiszai → Lillafüred

Local tram or bus to Felső-Majláth, then bus 5 or 15 to Lillafüred. Bus 5 is especially handy for St. Stephen's Cave.

⏰ Departure: The train ride itself is about two hours, but the whole journey takes longer with the transfer. Aim to leave between roughly 5:25 and 6:25, and check the connections for your exact date.
